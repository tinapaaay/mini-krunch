    Our Story

    It all started with a simple dream to make snacking exciting again.

    Learn how a simple idea, a lot of taste-testing, and a little chaos turned into your new favorite snack.

    1. 2021

      Back in 2021, with a little more time on our hands and a big craving for something new, KONU was born. What began as a fun kitchen experiment quickly turned into a passion project fueled by creativity, curiosity, and crunch.

    2. Today

      With the help of family and friends, we turned that dream into reality — and today, KONU is proudly found in over 400 stores across the Philippines and on shelves in major supermarkets in Canada.

    3. Every bite

      From our first snack to our ever-growing lineup, every KONU product is made with one mission in mind: to bring happiness in every bite and keep the joy of snacking alive.
