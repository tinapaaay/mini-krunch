    It all started with a simple dream to make snacking exciting again.

    How a simple idea, a lot of taste-testing and a little chaos became Konu.

    1. 2021

      Konu began in 2021 as a fun kitchen experiment, then grew into a passion project fueled by creativity, curiosity and crunch.

    2. Today

      With help from family and friends, that experiment became a brand. Konu says its snacks are now in over 400 stores across the Philippines and in major supermarkets in Canada.

    Every bite

    Konu describes one mission behind its growing lineup: bring happiness in every bite and keep the joy of snacking alive.
